CatchID Data Deletion

App: CatchID

Package: com.catchid.app

Developer/operator: Pete Colley

Contact: colley.apps@gmail.com

Deletion Instructions

CatchID does not currently provide user accounts, cloud catch-history storage, subscriptions, or cross-device catch-log synchronisation.

Your saved catch-log records remain locally on your Android device. Pete Colley cannot remotely access or delete locally stored catch-log records because they are not stored in a CatchID account or server-side catch database.

You can delete local CatchID data by:

  • delete individual catches inside CatchID;
  • clearing CatchID storage through Android Settings;
  • uninstalling CatchID to remove app-private local data.

Requesting Deletion of Data Processed Outside Your Device

To request deletion of data that may have been processed outside your device, email colley.apps@gmail.com.

Suggested subject: CatchID data deletion request

In your message, describe the data or activity concerned and provide an approximate date. Please do not send a fish photograph or other unnecessary sensitive content unless CatchID support specifically asks for it.

CatchID can attempt to identify and delete records that are reasonably possible to locate and within CatchID's control, such as support emails you send directly to CatchID and any related operator-controlled records.

What Cannot Be Deleted Remotely

Catch records, local photo references, locally generated share-card images, and other app-private data that exist only on your device must be deleted by you using the app or Android storage controls.

Gallery photos remain controlled by your selected gallery or document provider. CatchID cannot delete photos from a third-party gallery, document provider, email app, share target, or other app you choose to use.

Provider and Security Retention

Some provider or security records may remain temporarily because of operational, abuse-prevention, legal, or service-provider retention requirements. CatchID does not promise immediate deletion of records outside its direct control where provider retention or legal requirements apply.

Render Hobby dashboard logs are retained for 7 days and are intended to contain privacy-safe operational metadata only. No external Render log or metrics streams are configured.

CatchID sends store: false on OpenAI Responses API requests, but OpenAI standard abuse-monitoring records may still remain for up to 30 days, subject to legal and security exceptions.

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data retention is controlled through Firebase/Google settings and service behaviour.
